Section 4: God Records His Revelation The Bible is really a *library *
of books which spans the breadth of Salvation history. While God is its
true author, he worked through many different human authors to Reveal
himself. God inspired them to write those truths necessary for salvation
and to do so without error. However, these authors were free to use
their own Styles and discretion in doing so. The Jews and earliest
Christians believed certain writings clearly indicated that God's Sprit
had guided the authors in the truth. The task of Determining which
writings were inspired fell to the Church. The inclusion of the books of
the Old Testament meant that the Church appreciated that the Jews were
God's Chosen People which prepared for the coming of Christ.
